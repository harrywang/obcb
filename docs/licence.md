# Licence and redistribution

[← README](../README.md)

AABRI is **fully open access**, and the publication policy is explicit:

> All manuscripts published in AABRI journals are published online, open access. Manuscripts are
> available to the readers at no cost. Readers may read, download, and use the manuscripts and
> information therein for any lawful purpose without permission from AABRI.
> — [aabri.com/pubpolicy.html](http://www.aabri.com/pubpolicy.html)

So downloading them, and using their content to build a benchmark, is permitted outright. No
fees, no embargo, no permission needed.

**But open access here means free-to-read, not a Creative Commons grant.** Authors retain
copyright, and AABRI directs anyone wishing to *republish* to contact the authors
([aabri.com/copyright.html](http://www.aabri.com/copyright.html)). That is why this repo ships
`data/case_list.json` rather than the PDFs: fetching is covered by the reader grant, re-hosting
is not.

It also bears on publishing results. `benchmark.jsonl` contains reference solutions quoted from
the teaching notes, so releasing it would be redistribution of copyrighted text — that needs
author permission or paraphrased solutions, not just attribution.
